— Requirements —

What Arizona Law Requires

01

Auto Liability

Arizona requires drivers to carry at least 25/50/15: $25,000 per person and $50,000 per accident for bodily injury, plus $15,000 property damage.

02

Homeowners Insurance

Not required by state law, but virtually every mortgage lender requires it as a condition of the loan.

03

Umbrella Liability

Falls entirely outside any state-mandated minimum: a voluntary layer of protection, not a legal requirement.

— Coverage —

What We Recommend Beyond the Minimums

Higher Auto Liability Limits

The state minimum is far below what a serious accident can cost; most households carry $250,000/$500,000 or more.

Homeowners / Dwelling Coverage

Insured to full rebuild cost, not market value: rebuild costs have risen sharply in Arizona.

Personal Umbrella Liability

Standard policies cap out around $500,000 in liability; umbrella adds $1M–$5M+ above that.

Scheduled Valuables

Jewelry, art, and wine collections typically need to be individually scheduled: standard policies cap these categories low.

Boat, RV & Classic Car Coverage

Agreed-value coverage for collector and specialty vehicles that standard auto policies undervalue at claim time.

Flood Insurance

Standard homeowners policies exclude flood damage entirely: monsoon-season flash flooding is a real Phoenix-area exposure.

— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

Is homeowners insurance required by law in Arizona?

No, but any mortgage lender will require it as a condition of the loan, which is why most homeowners carry it.

How much umbrella coverage do I actually need?

It depends on your net worth and lawsuit exposure. Standard auto/home policies cap around $500,000; umbrella adds $1M or more above that.

Does my homeowners policy cover flood damage from monsoon storms?

No, standard policies exclude flood damage entirely, including flash flooding. That requires a separate policy.

What's the Arizona state minimum for auto insurance, and why isn't it enough?

Currently 25/50/15. A single serious accident can easily exceed those limits, which is why most households carry significantly higher limits.

Can you insure my classic car or RV at agreed value instead of actual cash value?

Yes, we place these on an agreed-value basis, so you and the carrier agree on value upfront, with no depreciation argument at claim time.
