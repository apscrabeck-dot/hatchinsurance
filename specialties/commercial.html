— Requirements —

What Arizona Law Requires

01

Workers' Compensation

Required for any Arizona business with 1+ employees (ARS § 23-901).

02

Commercial Auto Liability

Required for any vehicle titled to the business and used on public roads.

03

General Liability Insurance

Not required by statute for most businesses, but landlords, lenders, and clients routinely require proof of it as a condition of doing business.

— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance is my Phoenix business legally required to carry?

At minimum, workers' comp with 1+ employees and commercial auto liability for business-titled vehicles. GL isn't statutorily required, but most businesses carry it anyway.

What's the difference between a BOP and general liability alone?

A BOP bundles GL with commercial property into one cost-effective package. GL alone doesn't protect your building, equipment, or inventory.

Do I need cyber liability if I'm a small business?

Increasingly, yes: small businesses are frequent ransomware and data breach targets, and many contracts now require proof of coverage.

How much does small business insurance cost in Arizona?

It depends on industry, revenue, payroll, and coverages needed. Give us a call and we'll build an accurate quote based on your actual operation.
