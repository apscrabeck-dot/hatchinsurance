— Requirements —

What Arizona Law Requires

01

Producer Licensing & the Free-Look Period

Life insurance is not legally required in Arizona, but all policies go through a licensed producer, and state law guarantees buyers a free-look period to cancel a new policy for a full refund.

02

Business-Use Life Insurance

Key person and buy-sell life insurance are often required by a lender or partnership agreement, not by the state.

— Coverage —

What We Recommend Beyond the Minimums

Term Life

The most common starting point: income replacement for a set period (10/20/30 years) at the lowest cost per dollar of coverage.

Whole / Permanent Life

Lifelong coverage with a cash value component, used for estate planning or permanent needs.

Key Person Insurance

Protects a business against the financial impact of losing a critical owner or employee.

Buy-Sell Agreement Funding

Ensures surviving partners can buy out a deceased partner's share without a forced sale of the business.

Disability Income Insurance

Often paired with life coverage: protects income if illness or injury prevents someone from working.

— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

How much life insurance do I actually need?

A common starting point covers income replacement, outstanding debts, and future obligations like college costs: we can walk through a needs analysis with you.

What's the difference between term and whole life?

Term covers a set period at the lowest cost and pays out only if you die during that term. Whole life covers your entire life and builds cash value at a higher premium.

Do I need life insurance for my business, not just my family?

Quite possibly. Key person and buy-sell funding protect your business against the loss of an owner or partner, separate from personal life insurance.

Is life insurance required by Arizona law?

No, but lenders and partnership agreements sometimes require it as a practical condition of financing or partnership.

Can I get coverage if I have a pre-existing health condition?

Often, yes: different carriers price the same health history very differently, which is where multi-carrier access helps.
