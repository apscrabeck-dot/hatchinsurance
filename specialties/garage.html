— Requirements —

What Arizona Law Requires

01

Workers' Compensation

Required with even one employee (ARS § 23-901). Spray guns, heavy components, solvent exposure, and lifts create real injury risk.

02

Commercial Auto Liability

Required for any business vehicle on public roads: a personal auto policy will deny claims tied to business use.

03

Arizona MVD Garage License Bond

Required only for shops that also hold a dealer license. A standard collision or body shop without one is not required to carry it.

— Coverage —

What We Recommend Beyond the Minimums

Garage Liability

Covers bodily injury and property damage from your operations: a slip in the waiting room, a vehicle rolling off a lift, or a repair dispute.

Garagekeepers Legal Liability

Covers customer vehicles in your care, custody, and control. Garage liability and garagekeepers are not the same thing: most shops need both.

Commercial Property

Covers equipment, tools, and building on a replacement-cost basis, not actual cash value.

Business Interruption

Pays ongoing expenses and lost revenue while you're closed after a covered loss.

Umbrella / Excess Liability

Extra cushion above garage liability and auto limits, especially for luxury, exotic, or fleet work.

— Nuance —

Why Auto Body Insurance Is Different

Paint booth & solvent exposure

Standard property policies often exclude paint booths and solvent fires: you need a carrier that underwrites auto body operations specifically.

Customer vehicles aren't covered by standard GL

A standard BOP excludes property in your care, custody, or control: every car on your lot. Garagekeepers fills the gap.

Road test liability

Test-driving a repaired vehicle is a distinct exposure, covered only if your policy is written to include it.

Environmental & chemical disposal

Paint, solvents, and waste create environmental exposure depending on the scale of your operation.

— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does auto body shop insurance cost in Arizona?

It depends on revenue, payroll, employee count, and the value of customer vehicles on your lot. Give us a call and we can usually turn a quote around the same day.

Do I need garagekeepers coverage if I already have garage liability?

Yes, garage liability covers third-party claims about your operations; garagekeepers covers the actual customer vehicles in your care. Most shops need both.

What happens if a customer's car is stolen from my lot?

With garagekeepers, your insurer covers the loss. Without it, you're personally responsible for replacing that vehicle at full value.

My regular business policy covers my property. Does it cover customer cars too?

Almost certainly not: customer vehicles are third-party property in your custody, a separate exposure covered by garagekeepers.

Can I get insured if I just opened my shop?

Yes, some carriers prefer 2–3 years in business for their best rates, but newer shops can still get properly covered.
