— Requirements —

What Arizona Law Requires

01

Dram Shop Liability (ARS § 4-311)

A licensed establishment can be held liable for serving a visibly intoxicated adult, or any alcohol to a minor, who then causes injury.

02

Liquor Liability Insurance

Not mandated by statute, but most landlords, franchisors, and lenders require proof of it before an establishment can open or renew a lease.

03

Workers' Compensation & Commercial Auto

Workers' comp is required with 1+ employees (ARS § 23-901). Commercial auto is required for owned or operated delivery vehicles.

What We Recommend Beyond the Minimums

Liquor Liability

Responds to dram shop claims (over-service, service to a minor) that a standard GL policy excludes outright.

Assault & Battery Coverage

Kitchen fire is the most common cause of restaurant claims. Pair it with equipment breakdown coverage for coolers and freezers.

Employment Practices Liability

Umbrella / Excess Liability

Dram shop claims can be severe when serious injury is involved: most operators carry a substantial excess layer.

— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I legally have to carry liquor liability insurance in Arizona?

No statute requires it, but Arizona is a dram shop state, and most landlords, franchisors, and lenders require proof anyway, so it functions as a requirement in practice.

What's the difference between liquor liability and general liability?

GL covers standard bodily injury and property claims. Liquor liability responds specifically to over-service and underage-service claims, which standard GL excludes.

Does my policy cover a fight that breaks out at my bar?

Only if assault & battery coverage is specifically included: many hospitality GL policies exclude it entirely.

I was non-renewed after a claim. Can I still get covered?

Yes. We have access to excess & surplus lines markets that specialize in harder-to-place hospitality risks.

Do I need separate coverage for a patio or live events?

Often, yes: outdoor and event activity can expand liability exposure beyond what your base policy anticipates.
