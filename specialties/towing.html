— Requirements —

What Arizona Law Requires

01

Commercial Auto: Intrastate

Arizona sets minimum liability coverage for commercial vehicles, including tow trucks, operating within the state. Operating below state minimums puts your MVD registration and operating authority at risk.

02

Commercial Auto: Interstate (FMCSA)

If your trucks cross state lines for any commercial purpose (driveaway/towaway, repossession, or for-hire work), you need a USDOT number, MC authority, and federal minimum insurance.

03

Workers' Compensation

Required with any employees (ARS § 23-901). Hook-up operations, roadside exposure, and heavy equipment handling create real injury risk.

— Coverage —

What We Recommend Beyond the Minimums

On-Hook Towing

Covers the vehicle you're towing while attached to your truck: your basic commercial auto policy does not cover it.

Garagekeepers Legal Liability

Covers vehicles in your impound lot or storage facility. On-hook and garagekeepers are not the same thing: most operations need both.

Physical Damage

Covers your own trucks: collision and comprehensive. Liability coverage alone does not protect your equipment.

Motor Truck Cargo

For driveaway, towaway, or auto transport work: protects vehicles accepted into custody for hire.

Repossession Coverage

Must be explicitly written in. Repo work carries wrongful-repossession and confrontation exposure most towing policies exclude.

Umbrella / Excess Liability

An added layer above primary auto limits, given the severity of roadside and recovery-related claims.

— Nuance —

What Makes Towing Insurance Different

On-hook vs. garagekeepers confusion

They cover completely different things and are routinely mixed up by agents and operators alike.

Non-consent tow liability

Police-dispatched tows carry a different liability profile than consent tows: disputes and storage claims work differently.

Driver MVR requirements

Carriers scrutinize driver records closely. A new hire's DUI can spike premium or trigger a policy review.

Interstate vs. intrastate is not a technicality

Running unauthorized interstate routes can void coverage entirely and create personal liability.

— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does tow truck insurance cost in Arizona?

It depends on truck count, driver history, consent vs. non-consent towing, repossession work, and revenue. As a rough range, expect $3,500–$10,000+ per truck annually for a complete program.

What's the difference between on-hook and garagekeepers?

On-hook covers a vehicle while attached to your truck. Garagekeepers covers vehicles stored at your facility after the tow. Most operations need both.

Do I need FMCSA authority to operate in Arizona?

Only if you cross state lines for commercial purposes, including repo or driveaway work. Operating interstate without authority is a federal violation and can void coverage.

Can I cover repossession work under my regular policy?

Not automatically. Repo work has distinct liability characteristics and needs to be specifically underwritten and included.

My best driver has a DUI. Can I still get him insured?

It depends on the carrier and how recent the violation is. As an independent agency, we work with multiple carriers to find one that fits your driver profile.
