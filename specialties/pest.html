— Requirements —

What Arizona Law Requires

01

General Liability Insurance

Arizona requires pest control operators to maintain continuous financial security of at least $500,000 (ARS § 3-3615), most commonly satisfied with a GL policy.

02

Commercial Auto Liability

Required for company vehicles used on service calls.

03

Workers' Compensation

Required with 1+ employees (ARS § 23-901). Chemical exposure, ladder work, and crawl space entry all create real injury risk.

— Coverage —

What We Recommend Beyond the Minimums

Pollution Liability / Chemical Applicator

A standard GL pollution exclusion means chemical claims are excluded outright. This is the single most important coverage gap for pest control operators.

Errors & Omissions

Covers claims that a treatment or recommended program didn't perform as promised.

Equipment & Tools Floater

Covers spray equipment, tanks, and tools: theft from vehicle, damage in transit, or in the field.

Umbrella / Excess Liability

Chemical exposure claims can be expensive, especially for fumigation operators or commercial-property work.

— Nuance —

What Makes Pest Control Insurance Different

Standard GL excludes pollution

Pesticides and fumigants are chemical pollutants by definition: chemical claims are not covered without a separate endorsement.

Fumigation is a separate exposure

Most standard policies exclude or limit fumigation; it must be disclosed upfront and explicitly covered.

Termite treatment & WDI inspections carry additional bonds

An additional $100,000 surety bond or coverage rider applies to termite treatment and inspection reports.

Market practice runs above the state minimum

Most Phoenix operators carry $1M/$2M GL plus $500,000 pollution and E&O, well above the $500,000 regulatory floor.

— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance do I need to get my OPM license in Arizona?

Continuous financial security of at least $500,000: most operators satisfy this with a GL policy, plus commercial auto and workers' comp as applicable.

Does my general liability policy cover pesticide claims?

Most likely not. Standard GL contains a pollution exclusion, and pesticides are classified as pollutants: you need a separate pollution liability policy.

How much does pest control insurance cost in Arizona?

For one or two technicians, a complete package typically runs $3,000–$7,500 per year depending on revenue and payroll. Fumigation operators generally pay more.

Do I need to carry insurance for 1099 applicators I use occasionally?

You may still face liability for their actions under your OPM license: require them to carry their own GL and pollution coverage.

A customer is claiming my treatment harmed their pet. What happens?

With GL and pollution liability, your insurer investigates and defends the claim. Without pollution liability, the claim may be denied outright.
